Why the floor accounts for every later row

The tolerance is δ = 1/4, so an entry below (1−δ)s = 1/4 is low. With weights 2−n, the rows after the last low row N have total weight 2−N. Their density is at least one quarter each. That gives the floor 2−N/4. When no row is low, N is zero.

The bars show weight times density. The third display row groups all rows from n = 3 onward into their exact weighted average. Every individual row in that group meets the threshold.
